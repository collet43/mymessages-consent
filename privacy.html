myMessages Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

myMessages is a personal client-communication tool built and operated by Collette's Casino Services for its own business use. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

What information is collected

myMessages stores contact information (such as name, phone number, and email address) that the business itself enters for its own clients, along with a record of messages sent and, where a client has opted in, their texting consent preference.

How email sending works

If a Gmail or Outlook account is connected, myMessages uses that provider's own sign-in page to get permission to send email on the user's behalf. myMessages never sees or stores the account's password, and only ever sends messages the user composes — it does not read, store, or otherwise access the connected mailbox's inbox or existing messages.

Where data is stored

Client and message data is stored locally on the user's device and, for syncing between the user's own devices, in a cloud database (Supabase). It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Opt-in / opt-out

A client sent a text opt-in link can choose to opt in or out of receiving texts at any time via that link. That choice is recorded and respected by future messages.
